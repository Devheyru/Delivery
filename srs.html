SRS: Minoo Delivery Ordering Systems

1. Feature Overview

The ordering module is divided into two distinct flows to cater to "Express" buyers and "Bulk/Grocery" shoppers. Both flows rely on a Zone-to-Center-to-Destination logistics model.

Listing Types Supported

  • Menu Listing: Individual dishes for immediate consumption.
  • Hotel & Restaurant Listings: Specialized vendor-based browsing.
  • Fruit Listing: Fresh produce categorized for market-style shopping.

2. Ordering Mechanisms & Workflows

A. Single Item Ordering (Express Flow)

This workflow is used when a user clicks "Order Now" directly from a listing.

  • Logic: The system processes one menu_id and one vendor_id.
  • Distance Calculation:
    Segment 1: Vendor's Zone → Selected Delivery Center.
    Segment 2: Selected Delivery Center → Final Destination.
  • Pricing:
    Total = Item Price + (Total Distance × 50)
  • Checkout: User uploads one payment screenshot for that specific item.

B. Cart Ordering (Multi-Vendor Flow)

The "Collector" workflow. It allows items from different vendors and different delivery zones to be purchased in one transaction.

  • Aggregation Logic: The system sums the price × quantity for all items in the cart array.
  • Complex Logistics: The system identifies all unique vendor zones in the cart and fetches the distance from each unique zone to the chosen Delivery Center.
  • Combined Distance Formula:
    Total Distance = (Σ Unique Vendor Distances to Center) + Center to Final Destination Distance
  • Checkout: One single "Master Payment." User pays the consolidated total and uploads one screenshot to validate the entire cart.

3. Detailed Logistics & Pricing Formula

Minoo Delivery uses a Hub-and-Spoke model. Delivery passes through a "Delivery Center" for sorting.

Component Definition
Delivery Center The central hub where drivers collect items.
Vendor Zone The geographic location of the restaurant or fruit shop.
Final Destination The user's specific location (Office, Home).
Rate 50 Br per unit of distance.

4. User Dashboard Requirements

  • Order Type Badge: Clearly label if an entry was a Single Item or a Cart Order.
  • Item Breakdown:
    Single Orders: Show item name, image, and vendor.
    Cart Orders: Show an expandable list of all items in that transaction.
  • Logistics Summary: Display the chosen Delivery Center and Destination.
  • Status Tracking:
    • Pending: Payment screenshot uploaded, awaiting admin.
    • Processing: Payment confirmed; vendor is preparing.
    • Out for Delivery: Driver collected items from Center.
    • Completed: Items received by user.
